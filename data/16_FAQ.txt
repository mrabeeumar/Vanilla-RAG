StrideFlex Footwear – Frequently Asked Questions
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
________________________________________
1. Ordering
Q: How do I place an order?
A: Browse the website or app, add items to your cart, and complete checkout with your shipping and payment details.
Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A: Orders can only be modified within one hour of placement by cancelling and reordering. See the Order Cancellation document.
Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Gift wrapping is available for an additional $4.99 at checkout.
________________________________________
2. Shipping
Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5–8 business days; Express takes 2–4 business days. See the Shipping Policy for full details.
Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, StrideFlex ships internationally to select countries, with delivery times of 7–21 business days.
Q: How do I track my order?
A: Use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email or sign in to your account and view your order history.
________________________________________
3. Returns and Exchanges
Q: What is your return window?
A: Eligible items may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.
Q: How do I exchange for a different size?
A: Use the one-step size exchange option in your order history, described in the Exchange Policy.
Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the return is inspected and approved.
________________________________________
4. Products
Q: How do I know what size to order?
A: Refer to the Size Guide document for detailed measurement instructions and conversion charts.
Q: Are your shoes true to size?
A: Most styles fit true to size; category-specific sizing tips are listed in the Size Guide.
Q: Do you restock sold-out items?
A: Restocks depend on demand and manufacturing schedules. Sign up for restock notifications on the product page.
________________________________________
5. Payments
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: StrideFlex accepts major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, gift cards, and store credit.
Q: Is it safe to enter my card information?
A: Yes, all payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant, encrypted payment gateways.
________________________________________
6. Membership and Rewards
Q: Is the Rewards Program free?
A: Yes, membership is free to join and earns points on every eligible purchase.
Q: How do I redeem my points?
A: Points can be redeemed at checkout in $5 increments once you have accumulated at least 100 points.
________________________________________
7. Account
Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page and follow the emailed instructions.
Q: Can I order without creating an account?
A: Yes, guest checkout is available, though creating an account allows order tracking and Rewards Program participation.
________________________________________
8. Warranty
Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: A 6-month limited warranty covers manufacturing defects such as sole separation and broken stitching. See the Warranty Policy for details.
