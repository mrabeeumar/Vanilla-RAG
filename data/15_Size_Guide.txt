1. How to Measure Your Foot
1.	Place a piece of paper on the floor against a wall.
2.	Stand on the paper with your heel touching the wall.
3.	Mark the tip of your longest toe on the paper.
4.	Measure the distance from the wall to the mark in centimeters or inches.
5.	Repeat for both feet and use the larger measurement.
6.	Measure in the evening, when feet are typically at their largest.

6. Sizing Tips by Category
•	Running Shoes: Consider sizing up half a size to allow room for foot swelling during activity.
•	Hiking Boots: Order true to size when paired with thin socks, or size up when using thick hiking socks.
•	Formal Shoes: Order true to size for a snug, structured fit.
•	Sandals: Order true to size; adjustable straps accommodate minor variances.
________________________________________
7. Between Sizes
If your measurement falls between two sizes, StrideFlex generally recommends sizing up, particularly for athletic and outdoor footwear.
