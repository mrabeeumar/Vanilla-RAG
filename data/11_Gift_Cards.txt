StrideFlex Footwear – Gift Cards
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This document explains how StrideFlex gift cards work, including purchase, redemption, and terms of use.
________________________________________
1. Gift Card Types
StrideFlex offers two types of gift cards:
•	Digital Gift Cards – Delivered by email, available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, $150, and $200.
•	Physical Gift Cards – Available in select retail partner stores in denominations of $25, $50, and $100.
________________________________________
2. Purchasing a Gift Card
Digital gift cards can be purchased on the StrideFlex website or mobile app and are typically delivered by email within minutes. Customers may schedule digital gift card delivery for a future date.
________________________________________
3. Redeeming a Gift Card
To redeem a gift card:
1.	Add items to your cart.
2.	Proceed to checkout.
3.	Enter the gift card code in the "Gift Card / Promo Code" field.
4.	The balance will be applied to the order total.
Any remaining balance stays on the gift card for future use.
________________________________________
4. Gift Card Expiration
StrideFlex gift cards do not expire and carry no inactivity fees.
________________________________________
5. Checking Gift Card Balance
Customers can check their gift card balance by signing in to their account and viewing the "Gift Cards" section, or by contacting customer support with the gift card code.
________________________________________
6. Combining Gift Cards
Multiple gift cards may be applied to a single order. Gift cards may also be combined with one credit or debit card in the same transaction.
________________________________________
7. Lost or Stolen Gift Cards
StrideFlex is not responsible for gift cards that are lost, stolen, or used without authorization. Digital gift card codes should be kept confidential, similar to cash.
________________________________________
8. Non-Returnable
Gift cards are not eligible for return, exchange, or refund once purchased, except where required by applicable law.
________________________________________
9. Gift Cards vs. Store Credit
Gift cards are purchased directly by customers, while store credit is issued by StrideFlex as compensation for returns or service issues. Both function identically at checkout and never expire.
