StrideFlex Footwear – Customer Support
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This document explains how to reach StrideFlex customer support and what to expect from each support channel.
________________________________________
1. Contact Channels
•	Email: support@strideflex.com
•	Phone: +1 (800) 555-2026
•	Live Chat: Available on the website and mobile app during business hours.
•	Social Media: Direct messages on our official Instagram and Facebook accounts.
________________________________________
2. Business Hours
Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Eastern Time)
Saturday: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Eastern Time)
Sunday: Closed
Holiday hours may differ and are announced on the website.
________________________________________
3. Expected Response Times
•	Live Chat: Typically within a few minutes during business hours.
•	Email: Within 24 business hours.
•	Phone: Immediate during business hours; voicemail response within 1 business day outside of hours.
•	Social Media: Within 1–2 business days.
________________________________________
4. Priority Support
StrideFlex Rewards Summit tier members receive access to a priority support queue with reduced wait times. See the Membership Program document for eligibility.
________________________________________
5. What to Have Ready
To speed up your support request, please have the following information available:
•	Order number.
•	Email address used for the order.
•	Description of the issue.
•	Photos, if reporting a defect or damaged item.
________________________________________
6. Common Support Topics
Customer support can assist with:
•	Order status and tracking.
•	Returns, exchanges, and refunds.
•	Warranty claims.
•	Sizing recommendations.
•	Payment and billing questions.
•	Account access issues.
•	Membership and rewards questions.
________________________________________
7. Escalating an Issue
If your issue is not resolved through standard support channels, you may request escalation to a support supervisor. Escalated cases are typically reviewed within 2–3 business days.
________________________________________
8. Accessibility
StrideFlex is committed to providing accessible customer support. Customers requiring accommodations can contact support to request alternative communication methods.
________________________________________
9. Language Support
Customer support is available in English, with limited support in French and Spanish for select regions.
________________________________________
10. Feedback
StrideFlex welcomes feedback on products and services. Feedback can be submitted through customer support, post-purchase surveys, or product reviews on the website.
