StrideFlex Footwear – Order Tracking Guide
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This document explains how customers can track the status of their StrideFlex orders.
________________________________________
1. Order Status Stages
Orders move through the following stages:
•	Order Placed – Payment confirmed and order received.
•	Processing – Order is being picked and packed at the warehouse.
•	Shipped – Order has left the warehouse and tracking is available.
•	Out for Delivery – Package is with the local courier for final delivery.
•	Delivered – Package has been delivered to the shipping address.
________________________________________
2. How to Track an Order
Customers can track an order in the following ways:
1.	Sign in to your StrideFlex account.
2.	Open your order history.
3.	Select the order you want to track.
4.	View the current status and tracking number.
5.	Click the tracking link to view carrier details.
A tracking confirmation email with a tracking number is also sent once the order ships.
________________________________________
3. Guest Order Tracking
Customers who checked out as a guest can track their order using the order number and the email address used at checkout on the "Track Order" page of the website.
________________________________________
4. Tracking Updates
Tracking information is typically updated within 24 hours of a scanned event by the carrier. Some delays in tracking updates may occur, especially over weekends and holidays.
________________________________________
5. Delayed Tracking Information
If tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days, customers should contact customer support for assistance investigating the shipment.
________________________________________
6. Multiple Shipments
Orders containing items from different warehouses may generate multiple tracking numbers. Each shipment can be tracked independently from the order history page.
________________________________________
7. International Tracking
International orders may show limited tracking detail once the package enters customs processing in the destination country. Full tracking typically resumes once the package clears customs.
________________________________________
8. Package Marked as Delivered but Not Received
If a package shows as delivered but cannot be located:
•	Check with household members and neighbors.
•	Verify the delivery address on file.
•	Contact customer support within 7 days of the delivery scan.
StrideFlex will investigate with the carrier and provide a resolution.
