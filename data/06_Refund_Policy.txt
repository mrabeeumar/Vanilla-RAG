StrideFlex Footwear – Refund Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This policy explains how and when refunds are issued for eligible returns and cancellations.
________________________________________
1. Refund Eligibility
Refunds are issued for eligible returns that meet the conditions described in the Return Policy, as well as for approved order cancellations and confirmed shipping errors.
________________________________________
2. Refund Process
After a return is received:
1.	The product is inspected upon arrival at our warehouse.
2.	Our team verifies eligibility against the Return Policy.
3.	The refund is approved or rejected.
4.	Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
Refunds are issued using the original payment method whenever possible.
Depending on your bank or card issuer, funds may take several additional business days to appear in your account.
________________________________________
3. Refund Methods
•	Credit and debit card payments are refunded to the original card.
•	PayPal payments are refunded to the original PayPal account.
•	Gift card and store credit purchases are refunded as store credit.
•	Orders paid using a combination of gift card and card are refunded proportionally to each method.
________________________________________
4. Store Credit Option
Customers may choose store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method.
Store credit:
•	Never expires.
•	Can be used on any future purchase.
•	Is issued immediately after return approval.
________________________________________
5. Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued when:
•	Only part of an order is returned.
•	A returned item shows minor, non-disqualifying wear.
•	A restocking fee applies to a wholesale order.
________________________________________
6. Refund Exceptions
Refunds will not be issued for:
•	Products intentionally damaged by the customer.
•	Items returned outside the return window without prior approval.
•	Products missing major components.
•	Returns with fraudulent proof of purchase.
•	Items marked as Final Sale.
________________________________________
7. Shipping Fee Refunds
Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a StrideFlex error, such as an incorrect or damaged item. Return shipping costs paid by the customer are not refunded.
________________________________________
8. Cancellation Refunds
Orders cancelled within one hour of placement are refunded in full to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. See the Order Cancellation document for full details.
________________________________________
9. Refund Timing Summary
•	Return inspection: 2–4 business days after receipt.
•	Refund processing: 5–7 business days after approval.
•	Bank posting time: 2–10 business days depending on the financial institution.
________________________________________
