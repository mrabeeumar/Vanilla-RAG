StrideFlex Footwear – Discounts and Promotions
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This document explains how discounts, promo codes, and seasonal sales work at StrideFlex.
________________________________________
1. Types of Discounts
StrideFlex offers several types of discounts throughout the year:
•	Seasonal sales (e.g., Spring Clearance, Summer Sale).
•	Holiday promotions (e.g., Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Boxing Day).
•	Rewards Program member-only discounts.
•	Newsletter signup discounts.
•	Referral bonuses.
•	Clearance pricing on discontinued styles.
________________________________________
2. Applying a Promo Code
To apply a promo code:
1.	Add items to your cart.
2.	Proceed to checkout.
3.	Enter the promo code in the "Gift Card / Promo Code" field.
4.	Click "Apply" to update the order total.
Only one promo code may be applied per order unless otherwise stated in the promotion's terms.
________________________________________
3. Newsletter Signup Discount
New subscribers to the StrideFlex email newsletter receive a one-time 10% discount code, valid for 30 days on regular-priced items.
________________________________________
4. Stacking Discounts
Promo codes cannot generally be combined with other promo codes. Reward points, however, may be combined with a single active promo code.
________________________________________
5. Exclusions
Promotional discounts typically do not apply to:
•	Gift cards.
•	Items already marked as Final Sale or clearance.
•	Custom or personalized footwear.
•	Shipping fees, unless the promotion specifically includes free shipping.
________________________________________
6. Price Adjustments
If an item purchased at full price goes on sale within 7 days of purchase, customers may request a one-time price adjustment by contacting customer support with their order number.
________________________________________
7. Expired Promo Codes
Promo codes that have expired cannot be reactivated or applied retroactively to completed orders.
________________________________________
8. Employee and Partner Discounts
Discounts issued to employees or business partners are for personal use only and may not be resold or transferred.
________________________________________
9. Fraud Prevention
StrideFlex reserves the right to cancel orders or revoke discounts obtained through fraudulent means, including coupon code abuse or unauthorized distribution of member-only codes.
________________________________________
