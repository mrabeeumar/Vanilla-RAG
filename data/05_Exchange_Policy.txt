StrideFlex Footwear – Exchange Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This policy explains how customers can exchange eligible StrideFlex products for a different size, color, or style.
________________________________________
1. Exchange Eligibility
Customers may exchange eligible shoes within 30 calendar days of delivery, provided that:
•	The shoes are unworn and unused, aside from trying them on indoors.
•	The original shoe box and packaging are included.
•	All tags and accessories remain attached.
•	Proof of purchase or the order number is provided.
________________________________________
2. What Can Be Exchanged
Customers may exchange eligible shoes for:
•	A different size.
•	A different color.
•	Another available style of equal value.
If the requested product costs more, the customer will be asked to pay the price difference.
If the replacement costs less, the remaining balance will be refunded or issued as store credit.
________________________________________
3. Non-Exchangeable Items
The following items are not eligible for exchange:
•	Shoes marked as Final Sale.
•	Personalized or custom-designed footwear.
•	Gift cards.
•	Opened shoe care products.
•	Opened socks or insoles.
________________________________________
4. One-Step Size Exchange
For domestic orders, StrideFlex offers a one-step size exchange. Customers may request a replacement pair in a different size before returning the original pair. The replacement ships immediately, and the original pair must be returned within 14 days or the customer will be charged for both pairs.
________________________________________
5. Exchange Shipping
Exchange shipping is free for the first exchange on a given order. Subsequent exchanges on the same order may incur standard shipping charges.
________________________________________
6. Condition Requirements
Exchanged items are inspected upon arrival at our warehouse. Exchanges may be rejected if the shoes show signs of outdoor wear, damage, or missing packaging, in which case the item will be returned to the customer without replacement.
________________________________________
7. Exchanges for Manufacturing Defects
Shoes with confirmed manufacturing defects may be exchanged for the same style and size at no additional cost, even if they have been worn. See the Warranty Policy for details on covered defects.
________________________________________
8. Gift Exchanges
Shoes received as gifts may be exchanged for a different size, color, or style of equal value. Gift exchanges do not require the original purchaser's information, but the order number or gift receipt is required.
________________________________________
9. How to Start an Exchange
To request an exchange:
1.	Sign in to your StrideFlex account.
2.	Open your order history.
3.	Select the order you wish to exchange.
4.	Click "Start Exchange."
5.	Select the new size, color, or style.
6.	Print the shipping label if applicable.
7.	Pack the original shoes securely.
8.	Drop off the package at an approved shipping location.
________________________________________
