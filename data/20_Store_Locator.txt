StrideFlex Footwear – Store Locator
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
While StrideFlex operates primarily as an online retailer, select products are available through authorized retail partners in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.
________________________________________
1. Finding a Retail Partner
Customers can find nearby authorized retail partners by:
1.	Visiting the "Store Locator" page on the StrideFlex website.
2.	Entering a city, state/province, or postal code.
3.	Viewing a list of nearby partner locations with addresses and hours.
________________________________________
2. United States
StrideFlex products are available at select partner locations in major metropolitan areas, including New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, and Miami. Availability varies by store.
________________________________________
3. Canada
Authorized partner locations are available in Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and Calgary.
________________________________________
4. United Kingdom
Authorized partner locations are available in London, Manchester, Birmingham, and Edinburgh.
________________________________________
5. Australia
Authorized partner locations are available in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Perth.
________________________________________
6. Product Availability In-Store
Not all StrideFlex products are available at every partner location. In-store selection may be limited compared to the full catalog available online. Customers can check specific product availability by contacting the store directly.
________________________________________
7. In-Store Purchases and Returns
Purchases made at authorized retail partners are subject to that retailer's own return, exchange, and refund policies, which may differ from StrideFlex's online policies. Retain your in-store receipt for any return or exchange requests.
________________________________________
8. Corporate Offices
StrideFlex does not operate direct-to-consumer flagship stores at this time. Corporate offices are not open for retail purchases or in-person returns.
________________________________________
9. Requesting a New Retail Partner
Customers interested in seeing StrideFlex products at a specific retailer in their area may submit a suggestion through customer support.
