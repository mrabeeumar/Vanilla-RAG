StrideFlex Footwear – Return Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
At StrideFlex Footwear, customer satisfaction is one of our top priorities. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're happy to help. Please read the following return policy carefully before requesting a return.
________________________________________
1. Return Eligibility
Customers may return eligible products within 30 calendar days of the delivery date.
To qualify for a return, the following conditions must be met:
•	The shoes must be unworn and unused.
•	Shoes may be tried on indoors for sizing purposes.
•	The original shoe box and all packaging materials must be included.
•	All tags and accessories must still be attached.
•	Proof of purchase or the order number is required.
Returns that do not meet these requirements may be rejected.
________________________________________
2. Non-Returnable Items
The following products cannot be returned:
•	Shoes marked as Final Sale.
•	Personalized or custom-designed footwear.
•	Gift cards.
•	Shoe care products that have been opened.
•	Socks or insoles once opened due to hygiene reasons.
________________________________________
3. Condition of Returned Products
Returned products are inspected after arriving at our warehouse.
Returns may be rejected if:
•	The shoes show signs of outdoor use.
•	The soles are dirty or worn.
•	The product has been damaged by misuse.
•	The original packaging is missing.
•	Important accessories are missing.
Rejected returns will be shipped back to the customer.
________________________________________
4. Manufacturing Defects
Products with manufacturing defects are eligible for return even if they have been worn.
Examples include:
•	Sole separation.
•	Broken stitching.
•	Defective glue.
•	Material cracking under normal use.
•	Faulty eyelets or lace hooks.
Damage caused by accidents, improper cleaning, excessive wear, or misuse is not considered a manufacturing defect.
________________________________________
5. Return Shipping
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless:
•	The wrong product was shipped.
•	The product arrived damaged.
•	The item contains a confirmed manufacturing defect.
In these situations, StrideFlex will provide a prepaid shipping label.
________________________________________
6. Holiday Returns
Orders placed between November 15 and December 31 qualify for an extended return period.
These purchases may be returned until January 31 of the following year.
________________________________________
7. International Returns
International customers may return eligible products within 30 days.
Customers are responsible for:
•	Return shipping costs.
•	Customs duties.
•	Import taxes.
Original shipping fees are non-refundable.
________________________________________
8. Gift Returns
If you received shoes as a gift:
•	You may exchange them for another size or color.
•	You may receive store credit.
•	Cash refunds are not available for gift purchases.
________________________________________
9. Bulk and Wholesale Orders
Orders containing more than 20 pairs of shoes are considered wholesale purchases.
Wholesale returns require approval from our sales department and may be subject to a restocking fee.
________________________________________
10. Incorrect Items
If you receive the wrong product:
•	Notify us within 7 days.
•	We will provide a prepaid return label.
•	A replacement will be shipped as soon as the incorrect item is received.
________________________________________
11. How to Start a Return
To request a return:
1.	Sign in to your StrideFlex account.
2.	Open your order history.
3.	Select the order you wish to return.
4.	Click "Start Return."
5.	Choose the reason for your return.
6.	Print the shipping label if applicable.
7.	Pack the shoes securely.
8.	Drop off the package at an approved shipping location.
________________________________________
