StrideFlex Footwear – Shoe Care Guide
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Proper care extends the life of your footwear and helps maintain warranty eligibility. This guide provides recommendations by material type.
________________________________________
1. General Care Tips
•	Rotate between multiple pairs to allow shoes to fully dry between wears.
•	Avoid machine washing or drying unless specifically labeled as machine washable.
•	Store shoes in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
•	Use a shoe tree or stuff shoes with paper to help maintain shape.
•	Clean shoes soon after exposure to dirt, mud, or salt to prevent staining.
________________________________________
2. Mesh and Textile Shoes (Running, Walking, Casual)
•	Remove loose dirt with a soft brush.
•	Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap.
•	Air dry away from direct heat; never use a dryer or heater.
•	Avoid submerging in water unless the product is labeled washable.
________________________________________
3. Leather Shoes (Formal, Business Casual)
•	Wipe with a dry or slightly damp cloth to remove surface dirt.
•	Use a leather-specific cleaner and conditioner periodically.
•	Apply a leather protectant spray to guard against water and stains.
•	Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or heat sources.
________________________________________
4. Suede and Nubuck
•	Use a suede brush to remove dirt and restore texture.
•	Treat stains promptly with a suede eraser or specialized cleaner.
•	Apply a suede protectant spray before first wear.
•	Avoid water exposure, as suede can be easily stained.
________________________________________
5. Waterproof Footwear
•	Clean with a soft brush and mild soap; avoid harsh detergents.
•	Reapply waterproof protection spray every 2–3 months or after deep cleaning.
•	Allow full drying time before reapplying protective treatments.
________________________________________
6. Hiking Boots and Outdoor Footwear
•	Remove mud and debris after each use with a stiff brush.
•	Clean outsoles to maintain traction performance.
•	Condition leather sections periodically to prevent cracking.
•	Store in a ventilated area to prevent odor buildup.
________________________________________
7. Sandals and Slippers
•	Wipe down straps and footbeds with a damp cloth.
•	Air dry fully before storing to prevent mildew.
•	Avoid prolonged submersion in water for non-waterproof models.
________________________________________
8. Odor Control
•	Use moisture-wicking socks to reduce odor buildup.
•	Insert cedar shoe trees or odor-absorbing inserts between wears.
•	Replace insoles periodically for high-use footwear.
