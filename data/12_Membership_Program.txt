StrideFlex Footwear – Membership Program
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
The StrideFlex Rewards Program is a free loyalty program designed to reward customers for their continued support.
________________________________________
1. Joining the Program
Membership is free and open to all customers. Customers can join by creating a StrideFlex account on the website or mobile app and opting in to the Rewards Program.
________________________________________
2. Membership Tiers
The program includes three tiers based on annual spending:
•	Starter (Free) – Automatic enrollment upon signup.
•	Trailblazer ($500+ in annual purchases) – Enhanced benefits and faster point earning.
•	Summit ($1,200+ in annual purchases) – Premium benefits and top-tier perks.
________________________________________
3. Earning Points
Members earn 1 point per $1 spent on eligible purchases. Trailblazer members earn 1.25 points per $1, and Summit members earn 1.5 points per $1. Points are credited after the return window for the related order has closed.
________________________________________
4. Redeeming Points
Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases at a rate of 100 points = $5 in reward credit. Points may be redeemed at checkout in $5 increments.
________________________________________
5. Starter Tier Benefits
•	Points on every purchase.
•	Access to member-only promotions.
•	Birthday discount code.
________________________________________
6. Trailblazer Tier Benefits
All Starter benefits, plus:
•	Free standard shipping on all orders.
•	Early access to new product releases.
•	Increased point earning rate.
________________________________________
7. Summit Tier Benefits
All Trailblazer benefits, plus:
•	Free return shipping.
•	Extended 12-month manufacturing warranty on eligible footwear.
•	Priority customer support queue.
•	Exclusive access to Limited Edition Collections.
________________________________________
8. Tier Qualification Period
Tier status is calculated based on purchases made in the trailing 12 months and is reviewed at the start of each calendar quarter. Members who no longer meet the spending threshold are moved to the appropriate lower tier.
________________________________________
9. Points Expiration
Reward points expire if an account has no qualifying purchase activity for 18 consecutive months.
________________________________________
10. Referral Bonus
Members can earn 200 bonus points for each friend referred who completes their first purchase using a referral link.
________________________________________
11. Leaving the Program
Members may opt out of the Rewards Program at any time by contacting customer support. Opting out forfeits any unused reward points.
