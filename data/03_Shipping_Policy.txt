StrideFlex Footwear – Shipping Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This policy explains how StrideFlex processes, ships, and delivers orders to customers in supported countries.
________________________________________
1. Order Processing Time
Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.
Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed on the next business day.
During peak seasons (e.g., holiday sales), processing may take up to 3 business days.
________________________________________
2. Shipping Options
StrideFlex offers the following shipping methods:
•	Standard Shipping – 5–8 business days.
•	Express Shipping – 2–4 business days.
•	Priority Shipping – 1–2 business days.
•	International Shipping – 7–21 business days depending on destination.
Delivery estimates begin after the order has shipped, not after the order is placed.
________________________________________
3. Shipping Costs
•	Standard Shipping: Free on orders over $75; $6.99 on orders below $75.
•	Express Shipping: $14.99 flat rate.
•	Priority Shipping: $24.99 flat rate.
•	International Shipping: Calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.
________________________________________
4. Domestic Shipping
StrideFlex ships to all addresses within the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia, including most residential and business addresses. Delivery to P.O. boxes may be limited depending on carrier restrictions.
________________________________________
5. International Shipping
International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, and clearance delays that are outside of StrideFlex's control. These fees are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the order total unless stated otherwise at checkout.
________________________________________
6. Order Tracking
A tracking number is emailed to customers once the order ships. Tracking information can also be viewed by signing in to the StrideFlex account and opening the order history. Full tracking details are provided in the "Order Tracking" document.
________________________________________
7. Shipping Carriers
StrideFlex partners with major national and international carriers, including UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL, and Canada Post, depending on the destination and shipping method selected.
________________________________________
8. Delayed Shipments
Delivery delays may occur due to severe weather, customs processing, carrier disruptions, or incorrect address information. StrideFlex is not responsible for delays caused by circumstances outside its control but will assist customers in tracking delayed packages.
________________________________________
9. Incorrect Shipping Address
Customers are responsible for ensuring the shipping address is correct at checkout. If an order has not yet shipped, customers may contact support to request an address correction. Orders that have already shipped cannot be redirected.
________________________________________
10. Lost or Missing Packages
If a package is marked as delivered but cannot be located, customers should contact support within 7 days. StrideFlex will investigate with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, will provide a replacement or refund.
________________________________________
11. Multiple Shipments
Orders containing items from different warehouses may arrive in separate packages on different dates. Customers will receive separate tracking numbers for each shipment.
________________________________________
12. Signature Requirements
Orders above $300 in value may require a signature upon delivery for security purposes.
________________________________________
