StrideFlex Footwear – Order Cancellation Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
This policy explains when and how customers can cancel an order placed with StrideFlex.
________________________________________
1. Cancellation Window
Orders may be cancelled free of charge within one hour of placement. This window allows time for changes before the order enters processing.
________________________________________
2. After the Cancellation Window
Once an order enters the shipping process, it can no longer be cancelled. This typically occurs after the one-hour window has passed, as orders are sent to the warehouse for picking and packing.
Customers may instead:
•	Wait for delivery and return the order according to the Return Policy.
•	Refuse delivery, where permitted by the carrier, so the package is returned to StrideFlex.
________________________________________
3. How to Cancel an Order
To cancel an eligible order:
1.	Sign in to your StrideFlex account.
2.	Open your order history.
3.	Select the order you wish to cancel.
4.	Click "Cancel Order" if the option is available.
5.	Confirm the cancellation.
If the "Cancel Order" option is no longer visible, the order has already entered processing and cannot be cancelled online.
________________________________________
4. Cancelling Part of an Order
Orders containing multiple items may be partially cancelled within the one-hour window, provided the order has not yet entered processing. Contact customer support for partial cancellation requests.
________________________________________
5. Refunds for Cancelled Orders
Orders cancelled within the eligible window are refunded in full to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. See the Refund Policy for additional details.
________________________________________
6. Cancellations by StrideFlex
StrideFlex reserves the right to cancel an order in cases such as:
•	Payment verification failure.
•	Suspected fraudulent activity.
•	Product unavailability after purchase.
•	Pricing or listing errors.
Customers will be notified by email and issued a full refund if StrideFlex cancels an order.
________________________________________
7. Custom and Personalized Orders
Custom-made or personalized footwear cannot be cancelled once production has begun, typically within 2 hours of order placement.
________________________________________
