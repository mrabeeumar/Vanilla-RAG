StrideFlex Footwear – Warranty Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
StrideFlex stands behind the quality of its products. This policy explains what is covered under our manufacturing warranty and how to submit a warranty claim.
________________________________________
1. Warranty Coverage
Most StrideFlex footwear includes a 6-month limited manufacturing warranty beginning on the delivery date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including:
•	Sole separation.
•	Broken or unraveling stitching.
•	Defective adhesive or glue bonding.
•	Material cracking under normal use.
•	Faulty eyelets or lace hooks.
•	Zipper failure on boots that include zippers.
________________________________________
2. What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover:
•	Normal wear and tear.
•	Cosmetic scratches or scuffs.
•	Water damage from submersion or improper use of non-waterproof footwear.
•	Damage caused by pets.
•	Improper storage.
•	Unauthorized repairs or modifications.
•	Damage caused by accidents or misuse.
•	Odor or discoloration from extended use.
________________________________________
3. Extended Warranty
StrideFlex Rewards premium members receive an extended warranty period of 12 months from the delivery date on eligible footwear. See the Membership Program document for details.
________________________________________
4. Safety Shoe Warranty
Safety Shoes carry a separate 12-month warranty covering protective toe caps and slip-resistant soles, in addition to standard manufacturing defect coverage.
________________________________________
5. How to File a Warranty Claim
To submit a warranty claim:
1.	Contact customer support with your order number.
2.	Provide clear photos of the defect.
3.	Our team will review the claim within 3–5 business days.
4.	If approved, you will receive a prepaid shipping label to send the item in for inspection.
5.	Approved claims result in a replacement, repair, or refund at StrideFlex's discretion.
________________________________________
6. Claim Outcomes
•	If the same style and size are available, a replacement pair will be shipped.
•	If unavailable, the customer may choose a different style of equal value or a refund.
•	Denied claims will have the product returned to the customer with an explanation.
________________________________________
7. Proof of Purchase
A valid proof of purchase, such as an order number or receipt, is required for all warranty claims. Products purchased through unauthorized third-party sellers may not be eligible for warranty coverage.
